        Rules

        Whoever has posted the biggest bid for a genre sits at #1 for that genre. Ties are broken by whoever paid first.

        • Bids start at $5. There's no maximum.
        • Every bid is its own entry — to reclaim #1 after someone outbids you, post a new, higher bid.
        • Only the top 100 bids by amount are shown on the board — fall below #100 and your listing rotates off until you post a higher bid.
        • Rankings update automatically as soon as a payment is confirmed.
